Calculates four sides per drawer, excluding applied fronts and bottoms. Purchasing assumes nominal 1-inch thickness for 4/4 SAB maple. Stock-width utilization is estimated by ripping strips of drawer height. If a part is wider than the usable stock width, the estimate allows edge-gluing two or more strips. The whole-board count uses approximate two-dimensional packing of cut parts into finite stock lengths, including edge-glued panels and saw kerfs; real-world grain selection, defects, and shop cutting order may change the count.
