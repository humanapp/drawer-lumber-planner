Lumber & milling assumptions

Measured 4/4 SAB maple only. Edge trim reduces usable board width; extra panel width and extra part length increase rough blank dimensions. Minimum glue-up strip width applies to every strip in a glued panel.

Drawer bottom sheet cutting solver

For conventional drawers: bottom width/depth derive from outside box dimensions minus adjustable deductions. 5" boxes default to 1/4" bottoms; taller boxes to 1/2". Vertical pullouts need separate dimensions and are excluded. Check measurements on a prototype before cutting. Guillotine-compatible layouts account for saw kerf; optimization is heuristic.

Crosscut assignment solver

Multi-strategy optimization: compare complete plans, prioritize assigned panels, then fewer glue-ups, crosscuts, and less waste. Different panel widths and lengths may share a full-width crosscut; shorter rip lanes require trimming to final rough length. Labels such as A2-F identify drawer configuration A, copy 2, front panel; /1 and /2 identify glue-up strips. Segments are full-width crosscuts; hover to preview a drawer panel, or click a cut lane to keep its corresponding panel highlighted. Click again to clear. No cuts are finalized or recorded as completed.
